A Main Street built by families.

Grapevine has something most DFW cities don’t: a real, thriving Main Street. The shops, restaurants, and businesses that line it weren’t built by corporations — they were built by families who’ve been here for decades.

Beyond Main Street, Grapevine is a gateway city. DFW Airport brings global traffic. The Gaylord Texan and Great Wolf Lodge drive tourism. And along the Highway 114 corridor, a growing number of professional services firms and small businesses serve the entire metroplex.

Many of these businesses are approaching a generational transition. The founders who built them in the 1990s and 2000s are now thinking about what comes next. If that’s you, Decidedly Wealth Management can help. We’re a CEPA-certified advisory firm based about 25 minutes away in Fort Worth.

Common Questions

Exit planning FAQ for Grapevine.

I own a hospitality or tourism business. Does exit planning apply?

Absolutely. Hospitality businesses have unique valuation considerations — seasonal revenue, lease dependencies, brand value. We help you understand what your business is actually worth and how to maximize it before a sale.

My family has owned this business for two generations. What are our options?

Multi-generational businesses have more options than most people realize: transfer to the next generation, sell to a key employee, find an outside buyer, or structure a hybrid. We walk through every scenario in our Decision Lab process.

When should I start planning?

If you’re even asking the question, now is the right time. Most business owners benefit from 3-5 years of planning. But even a conversation today can clarify your options and give you peace of mind.
