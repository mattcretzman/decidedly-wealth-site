What is a CEPA?

Understanding the Certified Exit Planning Advisor designation — and why it matters when you're planning the most important financial decision of your life.

The Designation

CEPA stands for Certified Exit Planning Advisor.

A CEPA is a financial professional who has completed specialized training through the Exit Planning Institute. The designation represents a deep understanding of the unique challenges business owners face when planning their exit — and the tools to address them.

While many financial advisors can help with investments and retirement planning, a CEPA has specific expertise in the intersection of business value, personal wealth, and the emotional dimensions of leaving a company you've built.

Why It Matters

Exit planning requires a different kind of expertise.

Business Value

A CEPA understands how to assess, grow, and protect business value — not just financial assets. Your business is likely your largest asset, and it requires specialized attention.

Transition Planning

Whether you're selling to an outside buyer, transitioning to family, or executing a management buyout, a CEPA has the framework to guide you through every scenario.

Personal Readiness

Exit planning isn't just financial. A CEPA helps you address the personal and emotional dimensions — what life looks like after the business, and how to prepare for that shift.

Coordinated Approach

A CEPA serves as the quarterback of your exit team — coordinating with your CPA, attorney, and other professionals to ensure every piece of the plan works together.

What to Look For

Not all advisors are trained for this.

Most financial advisors focus on investment management and retirement planning. These are important — but they don't cover the full picture for a business owner approaching an exit.

A CEPA has been trained to think about exit planning holistically: business readiness, financial independence, personal fulfillment, and legacy. If your advisor doesn't ask about all four, they may be missing critical dimensions of your plan.
