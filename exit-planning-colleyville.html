Common Questions

Exit planning FAQ for Colleyville.

My practice is built around me personally. Can I even sell it?

Yes — but it requires planning. We help you build transferable value: documented processes, a team that can serve clients without you, and a transition plan that maintains client confidence. The earlier you start, the more valuable the practice becomes.

I’m a dentist/doctor. Do you work with healthcare practices?

We work with business owners across industries, including many in healthcare. The exit planning principles are universal — know your value, plan the transition, protect your wealth afterward.

How is Decidedly different from my CPA or attorney?

Your CPA handles taxes. Your attorney handles legal structures. We handle the big picture — connecting your business value, your personal goals, and your family’s future into one coherent plan. We work alongside your existing advisors, not instead of them.
