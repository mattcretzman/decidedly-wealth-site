Reply Management
________________
When someone replies, the automation stops. You pick up the conversation. Here's the approach:
* Keep it short. 2-3 sentences. Don't engage in long text conversations.
* Move to a meeting. "Sounds great - you free for coffee next week?" That's the goal. Every reply should move toward getting on your calendar.
* Use the book as a bridge. If someone shows interest but isn't ready for a meeting, send them the free book link. It keeps the relationship warm.
* Don't pitch in the DMs. Ever. The meeting is where the relationship starts. The DMs are just the door.
FROM THE KICKOFF: "Hey, cool to bump into a fellow Aggie. Looks like you're also local. Love to grab coffee sometime." That's the vibe. Every reply should feel like that.
