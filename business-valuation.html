The Gap

Your business is your largest asset. Do you know its value?

For most family business owners, their company represents the majority of their personal wealth. But unlike a stock portfolio or a piece of real estate, there's no ticker symbol to check. The value of your business is something you have to actively understand and intentionally grow.

We help you see your business the way a buyer or successor would — and work with you to close the gap between where you are and where you need to be for a successful exit.

What Drives Value

The factors that determine what your business is worth.

Financial Performance

Revenue trends, profit margins, and cash flow predictability. A business that generates consistent, growing cash flow is worth more than one dependent on a single contract or customer.

Owner Dependence

How much does the business rely on you personally? A company that runs well without the owner is significantly more valuable — and more attractive to buyers.

Market Position

Your competitive advantages, customer relationships, and reputation in the market. Strong positioning creates the kind of value that survives a transition.

Growth Potential

Buyers pay for the future, not just the past. Demonstrable growth opportunities — new markets, products, or efficiencies — increase what your business is worth.
