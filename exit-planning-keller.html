Common Questions

Exit planning FAQ for Keller.

I run a small local business in Keller. Is exit planning only for big companies?

Not at all. Some of our most impactful work is with businesses doing $1-10M in revenue. Every business has value — the question is whether you capture it or leave it on the table when you’re done.

What if I’m not ready to sell for another 10 years?

That’s actually the perfect time to start. The earlier you plan, the more you can do to increase your business’s value and reduce the tax impact of a future sale.

My spouse doesn’t understand the business. How do you handle that?

This is one of the most common challenges we see. Our Decision Lab process brings both spouses into the conversation — not with spreadsheets, but with the questions that actually matter: who are we, what do we want, and who matters most?
