    About This Calculator

    A starting point, not an appraisal.

    How this calculator works

    You enter one profit number: what the company earns before your own pay, interest, taxes and depreciation. The calculator picks a range of multiples for an HVAC company your size, adjusts it for the kind of work you do, and uses your readiness answers to place you inside that range. The ranges come from published sale data for HVAC businesses and private companies. The sources are listed for our compliance team and available on request.

    Why the answers matter as much as the profit

    Two HVAC companies with the same profit can sell for very different prices. Buyers pay more for maintenance agreement revenue, licensed technicians who stay, and an owner who is no longer running calls. They pay less for builder-heavy revenue and an aging fleet. Those are the questions in step 2.

    The number that matters most is what you keep

    The sale price is not your number. What you keep after taxes and debt is. Texas has no state income tax, so the estimate assumes a federal long-term capital gains rate unless you live elsewhere. Your actual tax depends on how the deal is structured, which is worth planning years before a sale.
