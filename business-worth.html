    What Drives Value

    What a buyer looks at.

    Financial Performance

    Revenue trends, profit margins, and cash flow predictability. A business that generates consistent, growing cash flow is worth more than one dependent on a single contract or customer.

    Owner Dependence

    How much does the business rely on you personally? A company that runs well without the owner is significantly more valuable and more attractive to buyers.

    Market Position

    Your competitive advantages, customer relationships, and reputation in the market. Strong positioning creates the kind of value that survives a transition.

    Growth Potential

    Buyers pay for the future, not just the past. Demonstrable growth opportunities, such as new markets, products, or efficiencies, increase what your business is worth.
