Who We Serve

Family business owners who want more than a financial advisor.

We serve people who believe their wealth should reflect their values — and who want a partner, not a salesperson.

Our Clients

We work with families, not just portfolios.

Our clients are family business owners — people who have built something real, who employ people they care about, and who want their wealth to serve a purpose beyond a number on a statement.

We are passionate about serving family-owned businesses because we believe stronger families create stronger communities and are positively good for the world.

Client Profiles

You might be a good fit if…

The Builder

You're growing a family business and balancing expansion with family financial security. You need help integrating and separating business and personal financial decisions appropriately — and navigating the unexpected challenges along the way.

The Planner

You're within seven years of your business transition. You're starting to think about what comes next — for the business, for your family, and for yourself. You want a plan, not just a hope.

The Steward

You've already exited your business and now you need a fiduciary partner to help you remain a wise steward of the wealth you've accumulated. You want your investments to reflect your values.

The Next Generation

You're stepping into a family business or inheriting wealth that carries responsibility. You want guidance that respects the legacy while helping you make it your own.

Honest Fit

We're not for everyone. And that's the point.

We don't try to serve everyone. We focus specifically on family business owners because that's where our expertise, our credentials, and our personal experience create the most value.

If you're looking for a day trader, a robo-advisor, or someone to chase the latest hot stock — we're not the right fit. But if you want an advisor who understands the weight of what you've built and will help you steward it with intention — we'd welcome the conversation.
