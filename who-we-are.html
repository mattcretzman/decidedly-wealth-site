Who We Are

Stronger families create
stronger communities.

We are passionate about serving family-owned businesses because we believe communities are better if there are businesses run by families, not corporations.

Our Mission

Enabling family businesses to strategically utilize their wealth in accordance with their values.

We focus on insightful decision-making as the key to success for family businesses. We aim to guide you in living a life that reflects your values today, tomorrow, and for future generations — ensuring a meaningful and enduring legacy.

Our expertise lies in supporting owners of mature businesses seeking to develop an exit strategy. We help create a tailored plan for family-owned businesses to develop personalized exit strategies and manage your assets after a successful exit.

Sanger D. Smith

Founder & Financial Advisor
BFA™ APMA® CEPA™

Sanger is the son of a financial advisor. He spent 10 years at his father's firm before founding Decidedly Wealth Management in 2022. A graduate of Texas A&M University with a degree in Agricultural Economics, Sanger focuses on helping family business owners navigate complex money decisions with lightheartedness and humility.

He is the author of A Life Rich with Significance: Transforming Your Wealth to Meaningful Impact and co-author of Decidedly Wealthy: 5 Key Decisions to Transform Wealth to Significance with his father, Shawn Smith. Together, they co-host The Decidedly Podcast.
