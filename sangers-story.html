Sanger's Story

He learned from his father.
Then he built his own firm.
Now he helps families build their legacy.

The Beginning

A financial advisor's son.

Sanger is the son of a financial advisor. He grew up watching his father, Shawn Smith, guide families through some of the most important financial decisions of their lives. He saw firsthand what it meant to serve people with integrity — and what it cost to do it well.

After graduating from Texas A&M University with a degree in Agricultural Economics, Sanger joined his father's firm. He spent the next 10 years learning the craft, building relationships, and developing a deep conviction about what families really need from their financial advisor.

Building Decidedly

A firm built on conviction.

In 2022, Sanger founded Decidedly Wealth Management with a clear purpose: to enable family businesses to strategically utilize their wealth in accordance with their values, fostering a flourishing legacy.

He named the firm "Decidedly" because he believes insightful decision-making is the key to success for family businesses. Not just financial decisions — but the kind of decisions that shape how a family lives, leads, and leaves its mark.

Credentials

The training behind the trust.

CEPA

Certified Exit Planning Advisor

The gold standard in exit planning — specialized training to guide business owners through every dimension of a successful business transition.

BFA

Behavioral Financial Advisor

A designation focused on understanding how emotions and behavior influence financial decisions — helping clients make choices aligned with their values, not their fears.

APMA

Advanced training in portfolio construction and wealth management — ensuring your assets are stewarded with discipline and purpose.
