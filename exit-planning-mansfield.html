Common Questions

Exit planning FAQ for Mansfield.

I own a manufacturing/construction business. Do you work with those industries?

Yes. We work with business owners across industries. Manufacturing and construction businesses often have significant equipment and real estate value that factors into the exit plan. We help you understand the full picture.

My partner and I don’t agree on when to sell. Can you help?

This is one of the most common situations we see. Our Decision Lab process creates a neutral framework for partners to align on timing, valuation expectations, and transition structure.

Is the first meeting really free?

Yes. No catch, no pitch. It’s a chance to talk about your business, your family, and what matters most to you. If we’re a good fit, we’ll talk about next steps. If not, you’ll still walk away with clarity.
